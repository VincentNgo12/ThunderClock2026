# InSite - Jobsite Safety Vest

## Pitch

Construction sites are dangerous places. According to the US Bureau of Labor Statistics: Workplace incidents cost the construction industry $10B+/yr in cumulative legal fees, reimbursements, insurance premiums and administrative costs. 

This figure does not account for the cost of maintaining safety at a jobsite: 
- Site supervisors
- Safety Inspections
- Incident reports 
- Site and worker downtime

According to OSHA's guidance on the business case for worker safety - companies stand to save $2 for every $1 spent on safety. 

So for those of you here for the business:

## Safety Saves Money
