Playback is real time unless you pick a slower speed or turn on Slow-mo. Slow-mo eases to 0.25× from 0.5 s before to 1.3 s after each fall event; while playback is slower than real time a badge shows the effective rate and the gravity it implies on screen, and the clock shows any rate other than 1×.

Follow tracks the worker 1:1 horizontally but follows height through a critically damped spring (about 0.8 s of simulated time), so in a fall the worker drops through the frame against the site, kept in view, and the camera settles about 1.5 s after the impact. Dragging orbits around the worker.
